[h1]Dams[/h1]
[b]Version 1.0.0, for Civilization VII 1.5.0[/b]
Dams adds a buildable Dam to Civilization VII. Build one on a river tile, navigable or minor, and floods on that river no longer pillage the settlements along it. In exchange, the river's floodplains are removed.
[h2]The Mod Includes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Three Dams, one per age: the Ancient Dam (Irrigation, 250 production, +2 Food and +1 Production), the Medieval Dam (Machinery, 450 production, +3 Food and +2 Production) and the Modern Dam (Electricity, 750 production, +3 Food and +4 Production).
[*]Placement on any river tile: a navigable river, or a land tile with a minor river. Towns can buy it with gold.
[*]One Dam per age on each river. A Dam from an earlier age doesn't block a newer one.
[*]Flood protection for every settlement that owns a tile of the dammed river, including other players' settlements. The settlement with the Dam is protected by the Dam itself. The others get a Levee, a hidden building that takes no slot. A settlement founded on the river later is covered from the next turn.
[*]A 3D model for each age, drawn across the river, and a build icon for each.
[/list]
[h2]Trade-off[/h2]
When a Dam is finished, the river's floodplains are removed from the map. Each of those tiles loses about 1 Food, permanently.
[h2]Limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Floods still happen, and each one still adds a permanent +1 Food or Production to one to three river tiles. The Dam prevents the damage, not the flood.
[*]Protection applies to the whole settlement, so a settlement on two rivers is protected on both.
[*]Units on a flooded tile still take damage.
[/list]
[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adds new buildings only. It doesn't replace base-game files or change base-game rows.
[*]Saves load with the mod on or off.
[*]Start a new game with the mod enabled. In a game already in progress, a Dam whose tech was researched before the mod was added isn't available.
[*]Single player. In multiplayer, a Dam protects only the settlement that holds it.
[*]Dams plus a mod that turns floods off: the Dam still dries the floodplains but protects against nothing.
[/list]
[h2]Languages[/h2]
English only for now. Ask in the comments if you'd like a translation.
